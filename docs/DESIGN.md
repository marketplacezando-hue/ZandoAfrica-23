## Visual Language
- Core visual signature: Zando Africa two-tone brand identity with emerald green (#00A651) action headers, warm orange (#FF8A00) highlights & CTA buttons, electric blue (#2563EB) call actions, and whatsapp green (#25D366) direct contact buttons.
- Material & depth: Clean layered elevation with soft card borders, gentle ambient drop-shadows (shadow-sm, shadow-md), pill-shaped tag filters, and a distinguished deep-forest-green sidebar (#0A2E23 / #0E3D2F) for the admin back-office.
- Containers & buttons: Rounded pill buttons (rounded-full) for top filters and call-to-actions, structured cards with badges for view counts and remaining days, clear separation between public portal and admin workspace.
- Layout rhythm: Responsive desktop and mobile layouts matching official mockups (VIP banner, popular products carousel, latest listings grid, 4-step ad publishing stepper).

## Animation
- Entrance: Subtle fade-in and slide-up for listing cards and modal dialogs (200ms ease-out)
- Interaction: Smooth hover and active scale transitions on action buttons (150ms ease-in-out)
- Scroll / transition: Horizontal scroll snap for category pills and popular products carousel

## Forbidden
- Generic purple-blue gradients or out-of-brand palettes
- Unstyled emoji in headers or navigation
- Text truncation without tooltips or broken price badges
